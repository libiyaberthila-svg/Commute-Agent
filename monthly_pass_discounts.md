# Commuter Pass and Discount Guide

- **Monthly Transit Pass**: Provides unlimited rides on Metro Blue Line and City Buses for $95/month.
- **Corporate Tax Benefits**: Pre-tax commuter benefits can reduce monthly travel expenses by up to 25%.
- **Rideshare Surge Policy**: When rain precipitation exceeds 70%, rideshare pricing increases by 1.3x to 1.8x.
- **Seat Reservation Window**: Express Rail bookings open 7 days prior and close 10 minutes before departure.
