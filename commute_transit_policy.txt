METRO AND RAIL TRANSIT COMMUTE GUIDELINES 2026
1. Peak Hours & Fares: Peak hours are 07:00-09:30 and 16:30-19:00 on weekdays.
   Express Rail fares include reserved seating in Cars 1 and 2.
2. Automatic Booking & Seat Preferences: Auto-booking is authorized for transactions under $50.00.
   Window seats and quiet car seats are subject to availability. Quiet cars strictly forbid phone calls.
3. Delay Compensation & Re-planning: If a train is delayed by more than 15 minutes, riders are entitled to
   a full fare refund or free transfer to parallel metro lines.
4. Luggage & Bike Rules: Foldable bikes and small luggage are permitted during peak hours.
5. Weather Disruption Policy: In heavy snow or severe rainstorms, bus speeds are reduced by 30%.
   Underground Metro Blue Line remains 100% operational during surface weather disruptions.
